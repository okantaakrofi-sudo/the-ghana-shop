# Product Images

Upload your product images to this folder with the following naming convention:

| Product | Filename |
|---------|----------|
| Premium Argan Oil Serum | `argan-serum.jpg` |
| 24K Gold Face Mask | `gold-mask.jpg` |
| Luxury Night Repair Cream | `night-cream.jpg` |
| Black Pearl Facial Essence | `pearl-essence.jpg` |
| Diamond Exfoliating Scrub | `diamond-scrub.jpg` |
| Platinum Eye Contour | `eye-contour.jpg` |

## Image Requirements
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Dimensions**: Recommended 500x500px (square)
- **File Size**: Keep under 200KB for optimal performance

Once you upload images with these exact filenames, they will automatically appear on your store!
